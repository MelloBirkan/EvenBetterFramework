# Stage 1: Scope the category

Narrow a broad interest to one niche you can research. The right size has named competitors to study and enough demand to matter.

- Too broad: "Health apps". Thousands of competitors and no clear way to stand out.
- Good: "Sleep tracking for anxiety sufferers" (audience plus problem).
- Good: "Habit tracking for fitness beginners" (audience plus niche).
- Good: "AI-powered journaling" (technology angle plus category).

## Questions

Ask only what the user's message has not already answered. Ask one closed question at a time with the host's question tool (see Question tooling in SKILL.md), recommended option first:

1. **Niche.** Offer 2-4 candidate niches built from the user's own words, each phrased as audience plus problem, with the one closest to the user's words first and marked recommended. The client's "Other" choice lets them type their own.
2. **Audience.** Consumer (Recommended: consumer apps are quicker to validate from App Store data) / B2B or prosumer.
3. **Angle.** No preference (Recommended: the gaps found in stage 4 show which angle pays) / AI-powered / simple utility / widget or system-surface first.
4. **Revenue goal.** Side income, USD 1K-10K a month (Recommended) / hobby, under USD 1K a month / full-time business, over USD 10K a month. A hobby project and a business need different evidence, so the goal changes how hard stages 2-5 push on revenue.

## Exit

Restate the niche in one line (audience, problem, angle, revenue goal) and get a yes before stage 2. When nobody can answer, do not wait for this yes: record the line as an assumption and go on to stage 2. Then create `.evenbetter/app-research/[niche-slug]/` in the user's project and start `research-notes.md` with that line.
