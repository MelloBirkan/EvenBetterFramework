# Stage 6: MVP concept brief

Write this only after the user has picked a concept in stage 5. It is not a full PRD. It gives enough to start building with any approach: SwiftUI, React Native, Flutter, Expo, a no-code tool or a freelancer.

## Brief structure

1. **Concept summary.** A 2-3 sentence pitch: what it is, who it is for, why now.
2. **Market context.** The gap you found, the key competitors and their weaknesses, and the revenue signals that show demand.
3. **Target users.** 2-3 personas, each with a name, context, pain point and what they would pay.
4. **MVP feature set.** 5-8 core features in logical groups. For each: what it does, why it matters, and one sentence on expected behavior. Mark each feature free or premium.
5. **Screens overview.** Every screen with a one-line description, grouped by navigation structure (tabs, flows). No wireframes, just the map.
6. **Monetization.** Free and premium split, price point, trial strategy, and why the price works against the competition. Show revenue after Apple's commission (`references/benchmarks.md`).
7. **Design direction.** Mood (minimal, playful, premium and so on), 2-3 hex color suggestions, a typography direction, and 1-2 existing apps whose look is in the right ballpark.
8. **Launch playbook.** 3-4 marketing channels ranked by fit, a week 1-4 launch plan, and one "gotcha moment": the single feature or experience that makes someone tell a friend.
9. **Risks.** The top 3 risks and how to reduce each.
10. **Success metrics.** 3-5 KPIs with specific 90-day targets.
11. **Sources.** Every URL used in the brief, grouped by section (market context, target users, monetization and so on), each with a retrieval date. The user will need these to refresh the brief when the data shifts.

## Exit

Save the brief as `[concept-slug]-brief.md` in the niche folder under `.evenbetter/app-research/`. Then suggest the next step: the evenbetter-ios-epic skill to plan the app's screens from this brief, or the evenbetter-app-launcher skill for pricing, App Store preparation and launch.
